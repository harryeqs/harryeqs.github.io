Hi there! I'm Qianshuo Ye (Chinese: 叶千硕), and you can also call me Harry. I've recently graduated from [Selwyn College, University of Cambridge](https://www.sel.cam.ac.uk/) with a Master of Engineering (MEng) and Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Information and Computer Engineering.

For my MEng project, I was fortunate to be supervised by [Prof. Bill Byrne](https://sites.google.com/view/bill-byrne/home) at the [Machine Intelligence Laboratory](https://mi.eng.cam.ac.uk/), Cambridge University Engineering Department, where I invesitigated the implicit visual grounding of Vision-Language Models in chart question answering. Currently, I am working with [Prof. Difan Zou](https://difanzou.github.io/) at the [School of Computing and Data Science, University of Hong Kong](https://www.cds.hku.hk/), exploring new frontiers in **Mechanistic Interpretability**.

# Research Interests
I am broadly interested in **artificial intelligence**, with a primary focus on **explainable AI**. I am also fascinated by **computational neuroscience**, especially how insights from the brain can inspire new architectures for learning and memory. Beyond these, I am also intrigued by the development of models with **abstract reasoning capabilities** and **AI alignment**.

# Beyond Research
I am a music lover and enjoy a wide range of genres, from classical to jazz and rock. I play the cello and bass guitar, and occasionally dabble with the guitar. Outside of music, I also enjoy running, cycling, hiking and experimenting with new recipes in the kitchen :)
