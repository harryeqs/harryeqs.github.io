Hi there! I’m Qianshuo Ye (叶千硕), also known as Harry. I’m an MPhil student in Advanced Computer Science at [Queens’ College, University of Cambridge](https://www.queens.cam.ac.uk/), working with [Prof. Mateja Jamnik](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~mj201/) on concept discovery for hierarchical concept bottleneck models.

Previously, I studied Information and Computer Engineering at [Selwyn College, University of Cambridge](https://www.sel.cam.ac.uk/), earning a Master of Engineering (MEng) and a Bachelor of Arts (BA). For my MEng project, I worked with [Prof. Bill Byrne](https://sites.google.com/view/bill-byrne/home) at the [Machine Intelligence Laboratory](https://mi.eng.cam.ac.uk/) in Cambridge’s Department of Engineering, investigating implicit visual grounding in vision-language models for chart question answering. In summer 2026, I worked with [Prof. Difan Zou](https://difanzou.github.io/) at the [School of Computing and Data Science, University of Hong Kong](https://www.cds.hku.hk/), exploring applications of **mechanistic interpretability**.

## Research Interests

I'm interested in making AI systems **more interpretable**, both by understanding *how models work internally* and by designing models and representations that are *intrinsically interpretable*. In particular, I'm drawn to **concept-based interpretability** and **mechanistic interpretability**. I'm also fascinated by **computational neuroscience**, especially how insights from the brain can inspire new architectures for learning and memory.

## Beyond Research

Outside of research, music is a big part of my life. My tastes span classical, jazz, and rock, and I play the cello, bass and guitar. I also enjoy running, bouldering, hiking, and trying out new recipes in the kitchen :)
